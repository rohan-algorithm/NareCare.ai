## Full description

Made for Indian women with PCOS and PCOD, or anyone who thinks they might have it.

NariCare gives you one simple daily plan, about 5 minutes a day, built around the food you actually eat: dal, roti, sabzi, mess thalis and tiffins. No quinoa bowls.

★ COULD I HAVE PCOS? TAKE THE FREE 2-MINUTE CHECK
10 questions based on the Rotterdam criteria doctors use. See your score, which signs match, and which tests to ask your gynaecologist about. It's a screening, not a diagnosis.

★ YOUR INDIAN PCOS MEAL PLAN
• Veg, eggetarian, non-veg, vegan and Jain
• North Indian, South Indian, Gujarati, Bengali or pan-Indian
• Home kitchen, hostel mess, tiffin or eating out
• One-tap swaps, protein target from your weight, YouTube recipes for most dishes
• Plate scanner: photo of any meal → PCOS score and one better swap

★ HOME WORKOUTS & YOGA WITH YOUR TRAINER
• 55 animated exercises and yoga poses: Tadasana, Butterfly, Malasana, Cat-cow, Bhujangasana and more
• 4 programs that get harder each week: Metabolic Kickstart, Insulin Strength Builder, Yoga for PCOS & Calm, Knee-Friendly Start
• Voice coach in Hindi and English, breathing cues in yoga
• Period days switch to gentle, period-safe stretches automatically
• No gym, no equipment needed

★ HONEST CYCLE TRACKING FOR IRREGULAR PERIODS
Most period apps assume a 28-day cycle. PCOS cycles aren't. NariCare shows a date window, not a fake exact date, and alerts you when a gap is long enough to see a doctor.

★ AN AI COACH THAT ACTS FOR YOU
Ask in Hindi or English. Say "I drank 2 glasses", "dinner feels heavy, swap it" or "remind me at 7" and it's done. Every change can be undone.

★ WATER AND MEAL REMINDERS THAT HELP
Reminders name today's dish and show how much water is left. Log with one tap from the notification. Discreet mode hides health words on your lock screen.

★ PROOF IT'S WORKING
Weight and waist, skin and hair scores, mood, cycle lengths, all on charts. A one-page PDF for your gynaecologist with your cycles, symptoms, labs and PCOS check.

★ LAB REPORTS IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
Snap your report. LH:FSH, insulin, testosterone, thyroid and vitamin D explained simply, with questions for your doctor. First report free.

FREE vs PRO
Free forever: daily plan, PCOS check, cycle tracking and alerts, water tracker, today's meals, 3 exercises per workout, 3 AI messages a day.
Pro (7-day free trial): full workouts and all programs, unlimited AI coach and Nari autopilot, 7-day meal plan with swaps, unlimited plate and lab scans, doctor PDF.
₹999/year (₹83/month), ₹299/3 months or ₹149/month. Pay with UPI, card or netbanking through Google Play. Cancel anytime in Play Store → Subscriptions.

PRIVACY
Your health data stays on your phone unless you choose AI features. Delete your account and data anytime in the app.

Built on the 2023 International Evidence-based Guideline for PCOS. NariCare is an educational companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always talk to your doctor before changing medicines.
