# NariCare — Product Thesis

**Tagline:** अपनी PCOS Journey को Control करो — Indian Diet, Yoga & Tracking  
**Package:** `com.naricare.pcos`  
**Audience:** Indian women 18–35 with PCOS/PCOD (diagnosed or suspected), Hindi+English, Android-first

---

## The real problem (not “another tracker”)

PCOS is chronic. Lifestyle is first-line treatment. In India, **only ~32% of women fully adhere** to diet/exercise plans. Research and competitor audits show why:

1. **Food mismatch** — Plans push quinoa bowls; women eat dal-chawal, mess thalis, street snacks. Taste mismatch → quit.
2. **No visible progress** — Symptoms improve slowly. Without proof, motivation dies in weeks 2–4.
3. **Life context ignored** — Hostel mess, joint-family kitchen, tiffin, late office meals — one generic chart fails all four.
4. **Lab literacy gap** — SRL / Thyrocare / Dr Lal reports are jargon. Women Google LH:FSH at 1am and panic.
5. **Time poverty** — 45-min gym plans don’t survive Indian schedules. Exhaustion + irregular periods compound it.
6. **Phenotype blindness** — Metabolic vs androgenic vs inflammatory PCOS need different priorities. Most apps give one plan.
7. **Clinic friction** — Expensive visits, no privacy, no shared history. Doctors get 10 minutes and zero structured data.

Existing apps (SwasthGO, Plate.Fit, Flo, PCOS Polly, AashaCare) each solve **a slice**. None close the loop: *phenotype → real-life meals → micro-habits → symptom/lab proof → doctor kit*.

## What “best” means for NariCare

**One sentence:** NariCare is the only PCOS companion that adapts to *how Indian women actually live*, then proves week-by-week that the plan is working — in Hindi and English — with a doctor-ready report in one tap.

### Shipped product surface (current build)

**Personalized Home** (your problem + day schedule/alarms) · Starter guide · Exercise alarm → Move Start (no photo) · Tonight’s plate · Flare care · Cycle calm · Progress Pulse · 2 micro-habits · Expert Council · Bad-day mode · Family scripts · Exam kit · Meds log · AI · Labs · Diet/Move/Track · Weekly · Doctor Kit · EN/HI · Trial

### Seven differentiators (must ship / must feel)

| # | Differentiator | Why competitors miss it |
|---|----------------|-------------------------|
| 1 | **Phenotype Engine** | Onboarding classifies metabolic / androgenic / inflammatory / mixed → daily priorities shift (insulin vs androgens vs inflammation), not a one-time quiz. |
| 2 | **Living Context Diet** | Modes: Family kitchen / Hostel mess / Tiffin / Eating out. Meals + swaps respect that mode. |
| 3 | **Progress Pulse** | Weekly closed loop: adherence × symptoms × cycle regularity → plain-language “what’s working”. Kills the #1 quit reason (no relief). |
| 4 | **Indian Lab Decoder** | Photo → OCR → Indian lab formats → Hindi+English plain talk + history timeline. Hero feature, not a buried upload. |
| 5 | **Cycle-synced micro-actions** | Max **2 habits/day** + **10–20 min** movement. Follicular / ovulation / luteal intensity auto-shifts. |
| 6 | **Doctor Visit Kit** | One PDF: cycle, symptoms, labs, meal adherence — built for a 15-min gynae consult. |
| 7 | **Privacy-first community** | Anonymous-by-default, India-specific topics (family pressure, marriage, fertility stigma), flag → human mod. |

### Explicit non-goals (MVP)

- Telemedicine marketplace (SwasthGO owns that; we partner later)
- Calorie obsession / food shame
- Diagnosis claims — wellness + education only; always “discuss with your doctor”
- iOS-first polish before Android Play launch

## Monetization

Hard paywall after profile setup. 7-day trial. Default **₹999/year** (₹83/mo framing); fallback **₹149/month**. Razorpay (UPI-first). No free tier — content quality is the moat.

## Success metrics (first 90 days)

- Trial → paid conversion ≥ 35%
- D7 retention ≥ 40% (habit apps usually ~20–25%)
- ≥ 3 symptom logs / week among active users
- ≥ 1 Doctor Kit export / paying user / month
- NPS ≥ 50 among women who completed Week 2 Progress Pulse

## Brand & tone

- Warm, confident Hinglish — never clinical cold, never Western “wellness guru”
